# Data Dictionary

## Raw
- `customers.csv`: customer master and acquisition attributes.
- `products.csv`: product master, pricing and margin assumptions.
- `orders.csv`: transaction fact table with revenue, discounts, costs, returns and contribution.
- `dim_date.csv`: calendar/time-intelligence dimension.

## Analytics
- `customer_360.csv`: RFM, CLV and churn-risk features.
- `product_profitability.csv`: SKU-level economics and returns.
- `category_profitability.csv`: category/subcategory economics.
- `cohort_analysis.csv`: acquisition cohort activity and revenue.
- `monthly_kpis.csv`: executive trend metrics.
- `state_performance.csv`: geographic performance.
